FOREVER FIT BOXING GYM - ADMIN APP COLOR SCHEME & LOOK & FEEL PROMPT

PRIMARY COLOR:
- Lime Green: #39FF14 (RGB: 57, 255, 20)
- Use this color for ALL primary elements, buttons, active states, highlights, and brand identity

COLOR APPLICATION:
- Replace ALL blue colors with lime green (#39FF14)
- Navigation bars: Lime green background with white text/icons
- Buttons: Lime green background (#39FF14) with white text, rounded corners (16-18px)
- Links/Text buttons: Lime green text color
- Input fields: Lime green focused borders (2px width)
- Checkboxes/Switches: Lime green active color with white checkmarks
- Progress bars: Lime green for active progress
- Selected states: Lime green background or text
- Icons: Lime green for active/selected states
- Badges/Tags: Lime green for success/active states

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Font family: Use modern, clean sans-serif fonts (Inter, Roboto, or similar)
- Headings: Bold (700 weight), clean and modern
- Body text: Regular weight (400), readable
- Button text: Semi-bold (600 weight), white on green backgrounds
- Text colors: White on green backgrounds, dark on light backgrounds
- Ensure high contrast for readability

VISUAL STYLE:
- Rounded corners: 16-18px for buttons and cards
- Shadows: Subtle shadows for depth and elevation
- Spacing: Generous padding and margins for clean layout
- Borders: Thin borders (1-2px) in lime green for focus states
- Gradients: Use lime green variations (full opacity to 80-90% opacity)

OVERALL AESTHETIC:
- Modern, clean, and professional
- High energy and vibrant (reflecting gym/fitness brand)
- Consistent use of lime green throughout
- White and dark colors for text/backgrounds
- Smooth transitions and animations
- Polished, premium feel

Apply this lime green (#39FF14) color scheme consistently across all screens, components, and UI elements in the admin app.
